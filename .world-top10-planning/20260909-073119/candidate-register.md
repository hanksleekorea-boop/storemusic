# 후보 레지스터

공식 페이지 접근과 제품 범위 적합성을 기준으로 10개 비교 후보를 고정했다.

- CAND-01 Rockbot: high confidence; Music starts at $25/month per zone plus a one-time media player. 100M+ fully licensed songs, hands-free scheduling, control from any device.
- CAND-02 Mood Media: high confidence; Fundamentals is listed at $17/month monthly or $12.75/month on yearly billing. Ad-free/licensed music, scheduling, central dashboard and a player option are listed.
- CAND-03 Pandora CloudCover: high confidence; Music plan is listed at $18.95/month or $16.95/month prepaid annually per location. 14-day trial, 1,500+ stations, AI stations, scheduling, no ads and business licensing are listed.
- CAND-04 SiriusXM Music for Business: high confidence; Service starts at $26.95/month with no long-term contracts. 240+ channels, licensing fees covered in the subscription, remote scheduling and messaging are listed.
- CAND-05 SoundMachine: high confidence; Business is listed at $29.95/month or $26.95/month annual; Premium tiers are higher. Create mixes, schedule content, program messages and manage users are listed.
- CAND-06 Soundsuit: high confidence; Curated starts at €29/month per zone or user billed yearly; Control starts at €39. 1,500+ stations, AI station creation, scheduling, crossfade and commercial licensing are listed.
- CAND-07 Mubert Business: high confidence; Business/public-space plan is listed at $9.99/month or $99.99/year. Commercial license, unlimited music, 24/7 broadcast and dedicated support are listed.
- CAND-08 Soundtrack: medium confidence; Official help center documents Essential and Unlimited music control options and a single-zone Starter mapping. The official API documentation describes sound zones and warns that client secrets must not be exposed in public apps.
- CAND-09 Music Your Brand: medium confidence; The service advertises a free 14-day trial and dashboard/schedule/one-click playback. It lists hundreds of moods and playback on PC, tablet, smartphone, Raspberry and set-top box.
- CAND-10 SHOPBGM: medium confidence; A Korean store-music checkout shows a 171,600 KRW VAT-included annual price for the selected example. The page states that performance and digital audio transmission rights are included in the product.

→ 이 목록의 뜻: 비교 대상은 실제 확인된 공식 자료가 있는 서비스만 포함한다.
