관찰일은 모두 2026-09-09이다. 가격·기능은 각 공식 페이지에 표시된 내용을 그대로 요약했으며, 순위·시장점유율을 의미하지 않는다.

- **SRC-01 Rockbot** — https://rockbot.com/pricing
  - Music starts at $25/month per zone plus a one-time media player.
  - 100M+ fully licensed songs, hands-free scheduling, control from any device.
- **SRC-02 Mood Media** — https://us.moodmedia.com/shop/solutions/audio/music-for-business-pricing
  - Fundamentals is listed at $17/month monthly or $12.75/month on yearly billing.
  - Ad-free/licensed music, scheduling, central dashboard and a player option are listed.
- **SRC-03 Pandora CloudCover** — https://cloudcovermusic.com/select-a-plan
  - Music plan is listed at $18.95/month or $16.95/month prepaid annually per location.
  - 14-day trial, 1,500+ stations, AI stations, scheduling, no ads and business licensing are listed.
- **SRC-04 SiriusXM Music for Business** — https://www.siriusxm.com/business
  - Service starts at $26.95/month with no long-term contracts.
  - 240+ channels, licensing fees covered in the subscription, remote scheduling and messaging are listed.
- **SRC-05 SoundMachine** — https://sound-machine.com/pricing?partner_reference=39720
  - Business is listed at $29.95/month or $26.95/month annual; Premium tiers are higher.
  - Create mixes, schedule content, program messages and manage users are listed.
- **SRC-06 Soundsuit** — https://soundsuit.fm/
  - Curated starts at €29/month per zone or user billed yearly; Control starts at €39.
  - 1,500+ stations, AI station creation, scheduling, crossfade and commercial licensing are listed.
- **SRC-07 Mubert Business** — https://business.mubert.com/docs/
  - Business/public-space plan is listed at $9.99/month or $99.99/year.
  - Commercial license, unlimited music, 24/7 broadcast and dedicated support are listed.
- **SRC-08 Soundtrack** — https://help.soundtrack.io/hc/en-us/articles/5003012145180-Learn-more-about-our-plans
  - Official help center documents Essential and Unlimited music control options and a single-zone Starter mapping.
  - The official API documentation describes sound zones and warns that client secrets must not be exposed in public apps.
- **SRC-09 Music Your Brand** — https://musicyourbrand.com/en/
  - The service advertises a free 14-day trial and dashboard/schedule/one-click playback.
  - It lists hundreds of moods and playback on PC, tablet, smartphone, Raspberry and set-top box.
- **SRC-10 SHOPBGM** — https://www.shopbgm.co.kr/bbs/payment_new2.php
  - A Korean store-music checkout shows a 171,600 KRW VAT-included annual price for the selected example.
  - The page states that performance and digital audio transmission rights are included in the product.
